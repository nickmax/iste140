Lesson 3: Netcode & Synchronization

Author: Fadil (Content Lead)

1. What is Netcode?

Netcode refers to the algorithms used to sync game states across players connected with varying network latencies. It masks delays so gameplay feels real-time.

2. Client Prediction and Interpolation

  • Client-Side Prediction: When you move, your computer updates your character's local position instantly without waiting for the server's response packet.
  • Interpolation: Smooths out enemy movement by blending positions between incoming data updates, preventing jittery player models.

3. Lag Compensation

When a player with higher latency fires a shot, the server rewinds time on its master clock to check where hitboxes were located when the player pressed the trigger. This prevents high-latency players from having to lead their shots unnaturally.
