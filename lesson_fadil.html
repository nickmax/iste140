Lesson 3: Netcode & Synchronization

Author: Fadil (Content Lead)

1. What is Netcode?

Netcode is the code that keeps players in sync over a network. It helps hide connection delays so the game feels responsive.

2. Client Prediction and Interpolation

  • Client-Side Prediction: When you move, your computer updates your character's position straight away instead of waiting for a reply from the server.
  • Interpolation: Blends positions between updates so other players move smoothly on your screen.

3. Lag Compensation

When a delayed shot reaches the server, it checks where the hitboxes were when the player fired. This helps account for the delay between firing a shot and the server receiving it.
