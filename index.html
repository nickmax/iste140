What happens when you press Play?

Behind the Screen explains how online multiplayer games work. This tutorial is for players and students who are curious about what happens when they join a match. You can follow the lessons without knowing how to code.

The four lessons cover different parts of an online game. First, you will learn how your device connects to a server and why ping matters. The next lesson explains how a game engine draws what you see on screen. Then you will look at how games keep players in sync when updates arrive late. The last lesson covers how anti-cheat systems help keep matches fair.

You can read the lessons in order or choose one from the sidebar. Each lesson explains the main terms using gaming examples. After reading, try the quiz to check what you remember. The references list has sources for further reading.
