What Actually Happens When You Press "Play"?

Every time you pull a trigger, press a key, or move a joystick in a multiplayer video game, your computer executes millions of calculations per second while firing data packets across oceans at nearly the speed of light. To the player, it feels instantaneous. To the underlying computer network, it is a complex balancing act of physics, mathematics, and software engineering.

This tutorial site breaks down the technology that powers modern online games into four fundamental lessons, explaining how servers handle connections, how game engines render frames, how netcode prevents lag, and how anti-cheat systems protect game integrity.
